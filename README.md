## 4) Tips to raise more

- **Post the first donation yourself**, or ask a close friend to — campaigns with an early donation get far more momentum.
- **Add 2–3 photos** to the story (use `run`, `medal`, `swim` from your files).
- **Pin a goal update** at milestones ("Halfway to Spain!") — it gives people a reason to re-share.
- **Thank donors publicly** (with their OK) — it nudges others to join.
- For close friends/family in Canada, mention they can also send an **Interac e-Transfer to ghanemabdo@gmail.com** (no fees — 100% reaches you).
